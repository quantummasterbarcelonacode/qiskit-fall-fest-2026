# The available challenges to do

Title and description sneak peek


**Quantum Generative Adversarial Networks**
In this challenge you will build a quantum generative adversarial network (QGAN) that learns to prepare the ground state of the critical transverse-field Ising chain. After a one-qubit warm-up, you will implement a parameterized circuit that respects the model's parity symmetry, train it against a Wasserstein-type critic that measures only local observables, and probe the result with entanglement and barren-plateau experiments. Then extend your QGAN in one or more directions: thermal states, a run on real IBM Quantum hardware, JAX acceleration, or learning time evolutions...

**Quantum Chaos & Scrambling in Transmon Networks** 
In this challenge you will treat quantum processors not as abstract logic circuits, but as complex, many-body physical systems; concretely, you will model superconducting transmon networks by mapping capacitive crosstalk to interacting spin Hamiltonians. Moving from 1D chains to realistic 2D architectures, the challenge pushes computational boundaries. To overcome the exponential "dimensionality trap" of a 25-qubit lattice, you will apply advanced physics techniques, like U(1) symmetry subspace projection, to drastically optimize their simulations. Ultimately, the goal is to compute dynamical spectral statistics and generate high-resolution phase maps to pinpoint the exact transition from integrable dynamics to true quantum scrambling.

**Simulating a Quantum Computer on Your Laptop**
How difficult are quantum dynamics to simulate classically? Can we do it with our own laptops? In this challenge, you will answer this question and find out what fundamentally limits numerical simulations of quantum many body systems by building full state vectors, sparse matrix solvers, and Matrix Product States (MPS). Throughout the process, you will reproduce a trapped-ion quantum computer simulation of a few qubits, track entanglement entropy across long-range spin chains, and push the classical simulators you will build to their breaking point!
