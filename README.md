# Qiskit Fall Fest 2026

## Welcome

This is the [Qiskit Fall Fest event for Master in Quantum Science and Technology](https://sites.google.com/view/qiskitfallfest26mqstbarcelona/). We're so excited to have you all here.

The [Qiskit Fall Fest](https://www.ibm.com/quantum/blog/qiskit-fall-fest-2026) is a collection of quantum computing events on college campuses around the world. Every Fall Fest event is organized and run by the students on each campus, with support from IBM Quantum. Student leaders work alongside IBM to design and plan their event, which are then run in the Fall on their local campus. The events vary from technical hackathons to beginner-friendly challenges, all with the goal of helping to grow the local community’s skill set.

The Fall Fest here on Master in Quantum Science and Technology is going to be a series of challenges, done in-person the weekend of October 3rd-4th.
Details about the hackathon and rules can be found below or in the Discord. Good luck!

## Hackathon Details

- All projects submitted must follow the challenges prompt, which we will reveal at the Opening Hackathon Ceremony on Oct. 3rd
- All projects must use Qiskit, the open-source SDK for working with quantum computers at the level of circuits, and application modules. You can [learn more about Qiskit and begin getting familiar here](https://qiskit.org/learn/).
- You can not start building your project before the Opening Ceremony, and you must submit your project by the deadline.
